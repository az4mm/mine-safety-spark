What is measured

Separate metrics for separate questions.

  • 1
    Observed helmet compliance

    Helmet-present worker-time divided by assessable worker-time. Unknown visibility is excluded and reported separately.

  • 2
    Incident episodes

    Consecutive observations of the same violation share an incident ID, avoiding frame-by-frame overcounting.

  • 3
    Analytics performance

    Synthetic or replayed records measure Spark processing. Vision accuracy requires separately labelled test data.

Alert priority rules

Illustrative project rules, configurable for a real site.

Medium
Visible missing helmet

In an area where a helmet is required.

High
Restricted-zone entry

A tracked person is inside a configured polygon.

Unknown
Insufficient visibility

A missed detection does not prove a violation.

These labels are priorities for human review, not accident probabilities.

Scope and limitations

Keep these points visible when presenting the results.

  • This prototype supports inspection and human review; it is not an industrial safety interlock.
  • Detection performance in unseen mine conditions requires independent validation.
  • Tracking IDs identify temporary camera sessions, not workers across cameras.
